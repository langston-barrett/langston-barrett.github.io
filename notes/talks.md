## Notes on *Some Rules for Making a Presentation*

- "Having periodic 'talk outline' slides (to show where you are in the talk)
  helps, especially for longer talks."
- "Put very little text on a slide; avoid text completely if you can."

## Notes on *How to Present a Paper*

- The point is to advertise the paper
- No formalism: "If your results cannot be described simply and informally, then
  there is no reason why anyone should be interested in them."
